# Religious Event Samples

**Footage note:** Indian religious processions and rituals; these are public sample recordings, not CCTV footage.

Adapted from the linked Wikimedia Commons sources. Each output was trimmed to 15 seconds, converted to 640x360 at 10 fps, stripped of audio, and encoded as H.264 MP4 for this demo. Original works retain their listed licenses. Adapted CC BY-SA works are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0; CC BY 3.0 and CC0 works retain their respective attribution terms.
